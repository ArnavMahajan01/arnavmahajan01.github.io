  • Perimeter

    Desktop Application

    Turn your desk into a trackpad. Perimeter listens through your laptop's built-in microphone and detects where you tap around it — left, right, front, or rear — then fires an action you choose: take a screenshot, launch an app, run a hotkey, open a URL, or execute a shell command. Eleven action types in total. A guided calibration (about ten taps per zone) trains a lightweight classifier for your desk, with optional speech and typing samples so talking and typing don't trigger it. Built-in evaluation tools report accuracy and latency with a confusion matrix, targeting ≥80% accuracy under 200ms. Everything runs locally — no audio is ever stored or uploaded. Python 3 with a React + shadcn/ui interface, packaged for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Free and open source.
